## Cross-cutting themes

1. **Legacy vault-path drift (context-vault v2 alias sunset)** — the single biggest cluster. Many skills still teach `user/`→`identity/`, `agent/`→`state/`, `projects/`→`plans/projects/`, `git/<slug>/`→`knowledge/repos/<slug>/`, `weekly|monthly/`→`journal/...`. CLAUDE.md keeps aliases **one minor release only**; these break at sunset. Affected: user-profile, reading, user-interview, context, gmail-lifestyle, project-doc, roadmap.
2. **Stale error/permission contracts** — old flat `{error,message}` envelopes vs the current `{ok:false,summary,errors:[{code,field,hint}],retryable}`; bogus status codes (422→400, a non-existent `409 already_running`); fabricated error strings (roadmap); a stale `403` claim (browser-history research/ is now writable).
3. **FTS5 query syntax** — docs-search teaches wildcards/booleans the sanitizer neutralizes.
4. **Bodyless / header-less curl examples** — wiki-trace/connect/lint POSTs that 400.
5. **Line-count ceilings** — schedule (241/246), user-interview (279/290), roadmap (213/216), attach (104/110), notion (88/95) sit just under their `skills-manifest.test.ts` `regressionCeiling` pins. Verbosity trims are needed to keep headroom AND to add the missing content above without tripping the pin.
